For Windows and Linux

Spot what takes up space.

Switch between a folder tree, a treemap, and charts. The largest folders and files stand out at once.

Rename a whole batch at once.

Combine rules for text, numbering, and case. Every new name is previewed before anything changes.

Clear out empty folders.

Find folders that were left empty, review the list, and remove only the ones you select.

Stays on your computer

Scans run where Dragabyte runs. Your files and results are not sent to us.

Reports to share

Export a scan as PDF, Excel, CSV, or HTML.

Also at home in the terminal.

Explore drives on servers and remote machines with the command-line version, on Windows and Linux.

Build and use the CLI ↗
dragabyte-cli

Explore the current folder

$ dragabyte-cli .

Write a JSON report

$ dragabyte-cli scan /data --json > usage.json
