Website

We and our hosting and network providers process connection data to deliver this website, diagnose faults, and prevent abuse. This includes your IP address, request time, requested URL, browser information, response status, and referrer when sent. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in a secure, reliable website.

This connection data is necessary to load the site. You do not need to create an account or submit personal details. Fonts and images are served with the site.

Analytics

With your consent, we use Swetrix on our instance at swetrix.pureportal.io to understand website use and diagnose problems. It records page paths, referring sites, campaign tags, language, time zone, section views, download and other link clicks, page loading times, and JavaScript error types and locations. Download events include the platform, package format, and version. Error messages and stack traces are not sent.

Analytics requests transmit your IP address and browser information to the instance and its hosting and network providers. Swetrix uses connection data to distinguish visits and derive device information and approximate location. We do not use tracking cookies, account identifiers, or session recordings. Page paths exclude URL queries and fragments; only the campaign tags described above are included separately.

Analytics starts only after you accept. Rejecting leaves it off; the website and downloads work either way. You can change your choice or withdraw consent through Privacy settings in the footer. Withdrawal stops further collection and does not affect processing that took place before withdrawal. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Your choice is stored in this browser until you change it or clear the site’s browser storage. This preference is used only to remember your decision and is not sent to Swetrix.

Downloads and app

Downloads and automatic update checks connect to GitHub. GitHub receives connection data, including your IP address and request information, and may process it outside the European Economic Area. See the GitHub privacy statement. You can turn off automatic update checks in Dragabyte’s settings.

The desktop app and CLI process your chosen files where they run. Scan history, settings, and rename templates stay on your device; reports are saved where you choose. Dragabyte does not send your files or scan results to PurePortal.

When you connect to a remote Dragabyte instance, folder listings, scan results, and requested file previews pass between that instance and your desktop. Use an SSH tunnel to encrypt that connection. You control which instances you connect to and which reports you share.

Following links to other websites, including PurePortal, is subject to the destination’s privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we process your address, message, attachments, and communication details to answer your request. Email is handled through Zoho Mail, provided by Zoho Corporation B.V. in the Netherlands. See Zoho’s privacy policy.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual or pre-contractual requests, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for our legitimate interest in handling other inquiries. Article 6(1)(c) GDPR applies where correspondence must be retained by law.

Retention and transfers

Connection and support records are kept for as long as needed to operate securely, resolve your request, meet legal retention duties, or establish or defend legal claims. They are then erased. App data and exported reports on your devices remain under your control.

Hosting, network, and email providers receive data needed for their services. Authorities may receive it where disclosure is legally required. Where our providers process data outside the European Economic Area, transfers require an adequacy decision under Article 45 GDPR or safeguards such as standard contractual clauses under Article 46. Contact us for information or a copy of the applicable safeguards.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions in Articles 15–21 GDPR, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction, and portability of your personal data. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it for the future. Send requests to support@pureportal.io.

You may complain to a data protection authority. The authority responsible for PurePortal is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Heilbronner Straße 35, 70191 Stuttgart, Germany.

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.
